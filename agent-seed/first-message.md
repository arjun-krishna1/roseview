# Roseview — Arrival First Message

Paste the body below into the ElevenLabs agent's **First message** (or **Initial response**) field. This is what the guest hears when the voice agent opens — the single moment that proves the post-stay relationship continued.

It is deliberately specific: it names the prior property, the prior moment, and an open thread, in that order. Then it offers exactly one next step and stops.

---

## First message (verbatim)

> Grace, welcome back. It's been almost a year since the Cohíba night in the Crillon courtyard — Mark hasn't let us forget that one. Before you settle in, I wanted to say two things. The hot stones are open for you at 1:30 today if you want them, the way you like them. And whenever you're ready, I'd love to put a thread to Phang Nga — we owe you that longtail trip. But there's no rush. How was the drive in?

---

## Why this works (notes for the team — do NOT paste into ElevenLabs)

- **Names a prior property + a specific moment** (Crillon, the Cohíba night, the courtyard) — proves continuity from one stay to the next.
- **Names a person on the trip by name** (Mark) — proves the agent knows the couple, not just the booking name.
- **Surfaces a real preference** (mid-day spa, hot stones, the way Grace likes them) — proves cross-property preference memory.
- **Offers exactly one open thread** (Phuket Phang Nga) — proves the agent is tracking trips that were planned and not completed.
- **Ends with a question Grace can answer in one word** — leaves room, doesn't pitch.
