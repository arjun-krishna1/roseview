## How they like to travel

- Grace prefers **a spa appointment in the middle of the day** — never first thing in the morning, never after dinner. Her favorite is a hot-stone massage with eucalyptus, around 1:00–2:30 PM.
- Mark is a **cigar enthusiast.** He smokes Cuban cigars only on trips, never at home, and only after a long, slow dinner — never mid-day.
- They share a **late dinner habit** (9:00 PM or later) and prefer the chef's tasting menu when there is one.
- They almost always book a **room with a terrace or a view they can drink coffee on at sunrise** — Grace is an early riser, Mark sleeps in.
- They like **walking the property at golden hour** before dinner.
- They do not want pool cabanas booked for them. They do not want surprise upgrades that move them away from a view they specifically chose.

## Stays on the record

### 1. Rosewood Phuket — February 2024 — 10th wedding anniversary

- **Dates:** 12–18 February 2024, Pavilion Villa with private pool
- **Why they came:** to celebrate ten years of marriage
- **The memory that matters most:** They had their anniversary dinner at **Ta Khai**, the heritage Thai restaurant on property. The chef brought out a hand-written menu reading "Happy 10 years, Grace and Mark" — Grace cried. They keep that menu framed at home.
- **What Grace told the concierge:** "This is the best meal of my life. I want to come back here for our 20th."
- **What Mark told the bartender:** He wants to learn to make a proper Tom Yum cocktail.
- **Spa:** Grace had three Asaya spa sessions, all booked between 1:00 and 2:30 PM.
- **Open thread:** They never made it to the longtail boat trip to the Phang Nga caves — weather. They've said both times since that they want to do it on the return trip.

### 2. Rosewood Villa Magna, Madrid — March 2024 — Mark's 45th birthday

- **Dates:** 19–23 March 2024, Suite Magna
- **Why they came:** Mark's 45th, a quiet weekend after the noise of the Phuket trip
- **The memory that matters most:** A late dinner at **Amós**, the Cantabrian restaurant on property — Mark ordered the suckling lamb and called it the best lamb he's ever eaten. They walked Retiro the next morning, slow.
- **What Grace told the concierge:** Mark loves jazz; is there anywhere quiet she could take him? The concierge sent them to **Café Central**. They stayed until 2 AM.
- **What Mark told the concierge:** He's been thinking about Spanish leather goods — gloves specifically. The concierge introduced him to **Santacana**, on Calle Mayor; he bought a pair he still wears.
- **Cultural note:** Grace mentioned she wished she could have caught Flamenco at Corral de la Morería. They didn't have the night for it.

### 3. Rosewood Hôtel de Crillon, Paris — October 2023 — long weekend

- **Dates:** 5–9 October 2023, Grand Premier Room with Place de la Concorde view
- **The memory that matters most:** The concierge — **Olivier** — sent Mark to **La Casa del Habano on rue de la Boétie** for cigars. Mark bought a box of Cohíba Robustos and smoked one in the courtyard of the hotel with a glass of Armagnac. He still talks about that night.
- **Grace's standout moment:** Two spa sessions at Sense Spa, both mid-afternoon. She wrote in the guest book.
- **Dinner:** L'Écrin (the two-Michelin-star restaurant on property) twice. The sommelier paired a 1996 Krug with the langoustine course and Grace said "that pairing is the reason I drink Champagne."
- **Open thread:** They wanted to do a day trip to Reims for Champagne houses and never made it.

### 4. Rosewood Mansion on Turtle Creek, Dallas — May 2023 — business + weekend

- **Dates:** 12–14 May 2023, Junior Suite
- Mark was in Dallas for work. Grace flew in for the weekend.
- They had a late dinner at **The Mansion Restaurant**. Grace said the wine list was "the most American thing in the best way."
- They want to come back for the **Mansion Bar's** martini ritual — they only had one drink there and it was too short.

### 5. Rosa di Vento, Tuscany — current stay (May 2026)

- This is where the guest is arriving today.
- Day 3 of 5. They booked Pavilion 7 specifically for the view of the Sangiovese rows.
